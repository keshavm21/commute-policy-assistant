# Zentrix Software Employee Transport Policy (fictional), version 2

## 1.1 Purpose and scope
This policy covers company-provided cab transport between home and the Zentrix Software office for all employees working on a fixed roster. It is owned by the Admin department and replaces version 1 in full.

## 2.1 Eligibility by distance
Employees whose home address is more than 4 km from the office are eligible for roster transport. Employees living 4 km or closer are not eligible and must arrange their own travel.

## 2.2 Eligibility by grade
Employees in grades L1 to L4 are eligible for roster transport. Employees in grade L5 and above receive a monthly travel allowance instead and cannot book roster cabs.

## 3.1 Booking and cut-off times
Roster pickups and drops must be booked or changed at least 4 hours before the shift starts. Requests made after the cut-off are not guaranteed.

## 4.1 Night-shift definition
A night-shift drop is any drop that departs the office between 8:00 PM and 6:00 AM.

## 4.2 Security escort for women employees
Any drop after 8:00 PM that carries a woman employee must have a security escort on board. A woman employee is never picked up first or dropped last on a night-shift route unless the escort is present.

## 5.1 Weekend pickups
Cabs on Saturday and Sunday are available only with approval from the reporting manager. The approved request must reach the transport desk by Thursday 6:00 PM.

## 5.2 Holiday pickups
No roster transport runs on company holidays, except for business-critical shifts approved in writing by the department head.

## 6.1 Ad-hoc trip approval
Ad-hoc trips outside the roster need approval from the employee's reporting manager before booking.

## 6.2 Airport trips
Airport trips are allowed only for official business travel and need approval from the reporting manager. A sedan is provided for airport trips.

## 7.1 Guest travel
A visiting guest may use a company cab when the host employee raises the request with HR approval at least one working day in advance.

## 8.1 No-show penalties
A no-show is when an employee does not board a booked cab and did not cancel before the cut-off. The first no-show in a calendar month results in a reminder email. Two no-shows in a calendar month result in a written warning. Three no-shows in a calendar month suspend roster transport for 30 days.

## 9.1 Cost caps
A single ad-hoc trip is capped at Rs 2,000. Any amount above the cap is paid by the employee.

## 10.1 Contact
Questions not covered by this policy go to the Zentrix transport desk.
