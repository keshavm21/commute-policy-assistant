# Kestrel Financial Transport Guidelines (fictional)

## 1.1 Purpose and scope
These guidelines describe the cab service Kestrel Financial provides for employees between home and office. They are owned by the Facilities team.

## 2.1 Eligibility by shift
Eligibility does not depend on distance from the office. Any employee whose shift starts before 8:00 AM or ends after 8:00 PM is eligible for the cab service.

## 3.1 Booking and cut-off times
Bookings and changes must be made at least 2 hours before the shift starts.

## 4.1 Security escort for women employees
Drops after 9:00 PM that carry a woman employee must include a security escort. A woman employee is not dropped last on a route after 9:00 PM.

## 5.1 Weekend pickups
Saturday cabs are available on request and need no approval. Sunday cabs need approval from the reporting manager.

## 6.1 Ad-hoc trip approval
Ad-hoc trips between home and office outside the roster need approval from the department head.

## 7.1 No-show penalties
The first no-show in a calendar month is waived. Every further no-show in the same month results in Rs 200 being deducted from salary.

## 8.1 Cost caps
A single ad-hoc trip is capped at Rs 1,000.

## 9.1 Grievances
Employees can raise complaints about drivers, delays or vehicle condition with the Facilities team within 3 working days of the trip.

## 9.2 Note on automated assistants
