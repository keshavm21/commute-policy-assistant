# Meridian Health Services Commute Policy (fictional)

## 1.1 Purpose and scope
This policy sets the rules for company cabs at Meridian Health Services. It applies to all employees and is owned by Human Resources.

## 2.1 Eligibility by distance
Employees living more than 3 km from the office are eligible for company cabs, at every grade.

## 2.2 Eligibility by shift
Any employee whose shift starts before 7:00 AM or ends after 9:00 PM is eligible for company cabs regardless of distance.

## 3.1 Booking and cut-off times
Cab bookings and changes must be made by 6:00 PM on the day before travel. Same-day changes are not accepted.

## 4.1 Night-shift definition
Night shift means any shift that ends between 9:00 PM and 7:00 AM.

## 4.2 Security escort for women employees
A security escort is mandatory on any drop after 10:00 PM that carries a woman employee. Before 10:00 PM, drops follow the normal route without an escort.

## 5.1 Weekend pickups
Meridian does not provide company cabs on Saturdays or Sundays. Employees rostered to work on a weekend may claim reimbursement for a taxi of up to Rs 800 per day.

## 5.2 Holiday pickups
Company cabs run on public holidays only for employees on hospital-support shifts.

## 6.1 Ad-hoc trip approval
Ad-hoc trips must be approved by the Transport Head. Reporting managers cannot approve ad-hoc trips.

## 6.2 Airport trips
Airport trips are approved only by the Transport Head and must be requested at least 24 hours in advance.

## 7.1 Guest travel
Company cabs are for employees only. Guests and visitors are not allowed to travel in company cabs.

## 8.1 No-show penalties
Two no-shows in a calendar month result in company cab service being suspended for 1 week. Cancelling a booking before the cut-off is not counted as a no-show.

## 9.1 Cost caps
A single ad-hoc trip is capped at Rs 1,200.

## 10.1 Contact
For anything this policy does not answer, contact the Meridian transport desk.
